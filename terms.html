Sharing your account breaks the Steam Subscriber Agreement, and Valve may penalise accounts it finds being boosted, including matchmaking restrictions, MMR resets or bans. We take care to keep a boost unremarkable (offline mode, chat off, a VPN matched to your region), but we can't control what Valve does and we can't guarantee your account won't be affected.

By ordering a boost you confirm the account is yours, that you understand and accept this risk, and that you'll keep Steam Guard enabled. Change your password once your order is finished. Replay analysis and coaching never need access to your account.
