FAQ

What do I need to send?

Just the match IDs, which you can copy from your Dota 2 profile or match history. We never need your Steam login.

Who analyses my games?

Players ranked well above the bracket they review, who have been checked on their own replays.

How do I get my breakdown?

By email as written, timestamped notes, or as a recorded video if you add the video extra.

Can I ask questions afterwards?

Yes. Every package includes one follow-up question, and the deep dive includes a live call.

What if my replay has expired?

Replays can only be downloaded for a limited time. If one has expired, we'll ask you for another match.
